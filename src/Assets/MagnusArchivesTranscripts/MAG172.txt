There is no escape to be found here, no respite from the charade that is now
the sum of Francis’ existence. By now, Francis knows with utter clarity what
falling to the call will bring, the awful crawling fate that they will endure before
the next act eventually begins.

The cork of the bottle moves ever so slightly proud of the top, pushed by the
unfurling thing inside. The cigarette scuttles closer, inch by impatient inch.
Their longing is awful. And mutual. Pause.

and real, not this dull, unending ache that does nothing but propel them
towards one grotesque act of consumption after another, but… for all their
